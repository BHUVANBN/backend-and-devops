Computer network - A telecommunication framework that allows digital devices to communicate, Computer connected together
Internet - collection of Computer Networks
______________________________________________________________________________________________
Data communication requires 5 components - Message, Sender, Receiver, Transmission medium, Protocol 
Effectiveness of the data commuication - Delivery to dest, Accuracy of data, Timeliness, Jitter(delay between packet arrival time)
Transmission mode Categories - 
  simplex(one way commuication eg: radio), 
  Half Duplex(two way commuication but one at a time, eg: walkie talkie), 
  Full duplex(tow way communication at same time)
Network criterias - Performance, reliability, Security
Types of connection - Point to Point, Multipoint
Physical Topologies - Bus, Ring, Star(used in organizations), Tree, Mesh, Hybrid
